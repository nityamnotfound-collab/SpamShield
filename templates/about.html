ABOUT THIS PROJECT

A clearer way to pause and check

SpamShield is an academic tool that helps people review suspicious messages and URLs before taking action.

What SpamShield does

You manually paste a message or URL, and SpamShield returns an automated estimate with patterns that may deserve attention. It does not access inboxes. Submitted URLs are analyzed as text and are never opened.

Why spam detection matters

Scam messages can pressure people to reveal passwords, OTPs, payment details, or personal information. A second look at unexpected urgency, rewards, account warnings, and links can help people pause and verify a request independently.

How the model works

TF-IDF turns words and short word combinations into numbers. It gives more weight to terms that are useful for distinguishing messages and less weight to terms that appear everywhere.

Multinomial Naive Bayes learns which word patterns tend to appear in spam or legitimate examples. For a new message, it estimates how likely the message is to belong to each class based on the patterns it learned.

In short: TF-IDF represents the text as useful numbers, and Naive Bayes uses those numbers to estimate a class.

Limits of the system

SpamShield can make mistakes. Language changes, examples may not represent every scam, and a safe-looking message may still be harmful. A classification is an estimate, not proof that a message is safe or malicious. SpamShield does not verify senders or check URLs against a threat-intelligence database.

Tips for protecting yourself

  • Do not share OTPs, passwords, UPI PINs, or card security codes.
  • Be cautious of unexpected urgency, prizes, refunds, or job offers that require a fee.
  • Do not use contact details or links supplied by a suspicious message; independently find the official channel.
  • Check a URL's spelling and destination carefully before deciding whether to open it.
  • If a message claims to be from a bank or delivery service, verify it through its official app or known customer-support contact.

Use SpamShield as an awareness aid, and independently verify suspicious messages.
