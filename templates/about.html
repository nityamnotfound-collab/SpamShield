ABOUT THIS PROJECT

A moment to pause and check

SpamShield is a student project that helps people review suspicious messages and links before deciding what to do next.

What SpamShield does

It analyses text you choose to submit and returns an automated estimate with patterns that may deserve attention. It does not access inboxes, verify senders, or open links.

Why a second look helps

Unexpected urgency, account warnings, prizes, and requests for money or private details can pressure people into acting quickly. Pause and verify requests independently.

How message checks work

A text classifier looks for word patterns learned from examples. Separate rule-based checks can highlight common warning signs. Link checks use local patterns in the URL text only.

Important limitations

SpamShield can make mistakes. A safe-looking result is not proof that a message is genuine, and a warning is not proof of fraud. The service does not check links against an external threat database.

Protect your information

  • Never share OTPs, passwords, UPI PINs, or card security codes.
  • Contact an organization through its official app, website, or a known phone number.
  • Do not pay a fee to claim an unexpected prize or job.
